Privacy Policy

How MTARISE CONSTRUCTION LTD handles information from this website.

Last updated: 12 August 2026. This website is operated by MTARISE CONSTRUCTION LTD. This page is intended as website information and should be reviewed with a qualified Irish solicitor for your specific legal requirements.

1. Who we are

MTARISE CONSTRUCTION LTD is a general contractor providing construction, renovation, MEP coordination and maintenance services across Ireland.

Contact: anto@mtarise.com · 087 453 3339 · 3 Park Place, Grange Rath, Drogheda, Ireland.

2. Information you choose to provide

If you request a quotation or contact us, you may provide your name, email address, phone number, property address, project details, preferred contact method and images or documents relating to your project.

This website uses email links and your device’s email or sharing application to send requests to anto@mtarise.com. The website does not operate a server-side customer database or online account system.

3. How we use information

  • To respond to enquiries and quote requests.
  • To discuss, plan and deliver requested construction services.
  • To arrange site visits, provide estimates and communicate project updates.
  • To keep appropriate business, accounting and contractual records.
  • To protect the security and operation of the website.

4. Sharing information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share relevant project information with trusted professional or certified specialists where this is necessary to assess, coordinate or deliver work you have requested. We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect our legal rights.

5. Email, hosting and third-party services

When you use an email link, your email provider processes the message. The website is hosted through a web hosting provider, which may process basic technical information such as IP address, browser type and request logs for security and service operation. The website also loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may cause your browser to connect to Google’s servers.

6. Retention and security

We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose it was collected, including responding to enquiries, managing projects, meeting legal obligations and resolving disputes. We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information, but no internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your rights

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of or restriction of use of your personal information, and to object to certain processing. To make a request, email anto@mtarise.com. You may also contact the Irish Data Protection Commission if you have a concern.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the website, our services or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with the updated date above.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact anto@mtarise.com.

General Contractor specialising in residential construction, renovation, MEP, and maintenance services across Ireland.
